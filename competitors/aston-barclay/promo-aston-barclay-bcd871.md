# Aston Barclay Tiered Buyer Fees, Assured Inspection Guarantee & BCA Network Integration

Aston Barclay provides wholesale remarketing services across physical auction centres and digital channels, operating a volume-tiered fee schedule and structured condition guarantees, with physical and digital operations integrated under the Constellation Automotive Group (BCA) ecosystem.[^bca-constellation-network]

## Core Buyer Incentives & Operational Structure
* **Incentive Type:** Tiered Volume Buyer Fee Discounts, Inspection Guarantee & Digital Remarketing
* **Tiered Buyer Fee Structure:**
  * Volume-tiered buyer fee model spanning progressive purchase bands (from Standard to Elite), enabling high-turnover dealerships to unlock lower per-unit buyer premiums.[^aston-barclay-portal]
  * Fees calculate against the gross hammer price, with rates aligned across unified Constellation/BCA network trading terms.
* **Risk Mitigation & Condition Guarantee (Aston Barclay Assured):**
  * **Standard Assured:** 31-point visual and mechanical condition assessment covering fluid levels, engine start, transmission, and braking performance via a 20-metre kinetic drive test.[^aston-barclay-portal]
  * **EV & Hybrid Assured:** Expanded 35-point check including high-voltage charging cable checks and battery/drive indicators.
  * **Fault Rectification:** Discrepancy claims submitted within 2 working days and under 250 miles of transport/collection cover agreed repair costs on undisclosed defects.[^aston-barclay-portal]
  * **Assured Lite:** Lower-cost (£10+VAT) informational check for vehicles over 12 years or 120,000 miles.

## Auction Mechanics & 24/7 Digital Channels
* **e-Xchange & Cascade System:** 24/7 digital wholesale exchange allowing trade buyers to purchase or bid on fresh inventory upstream before vehicles cascade into timed digital sales or physical lane auctions.[^aston-barclay-portal]
* **Logistics & Delivery:** Nationwide vehicle transport ranging from single-vehicle plate delivery to multi-car transporter drops, supported by real-time telematics and digital condition verification at handover.[^aston-barclay-portal]

## Strategic Market Note
Following acquisition by Constellation Automotive Group, Aston Barclay physical auction locations and digital remarketing tools coordinate under the broader BCA operational and logistics umbrella, giving buyers access to unified inventory management and transport pipelines.[^bca-constellation-network]
