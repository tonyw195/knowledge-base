# BCA Tiered Volume Loyalty Scheme & Consolidated Multi-Vehicle Logistics

British Car Auctions (BCA) operates an account-tiered loyalty volume structure (Blue, Silver, Gold, Platinum) designed to retain high-volume remarketing trade buyers through scaled fee discounts, logistics concessions, and extended site grace periods.[^bca-buyer-portal]

## Core Buyer Incentives & Volume Tiers
* **Incentive Type:** Tiered Volume Buyer Fee Discount & Consolidated Transport Booking
* **Tier Structure (Rolling 12-Month Volume):**
  * *Standard / Blue Tier:* Baseline entry tier for occasional buyers; incurs highest scale of buyer's premium fees.[^bca-buyer-portal]
  * *Silver Tier (~75+ units/year):* Step-down reduction in per-vehicle buyer premium fees.[^bca-transport-logistics]
  * *Gold & Platinum Tiers (~300–1,000+ units/year):* Maximum reduction in buyer fee schedules per hammer price band, dedicated account managers, and extended collection/storage grace windows.[^bca-buyer-portal]
* **Consolidated Logistics Incentive (MyBCA Delivery):**
  * Trade buyers can consolidate delivery bookings for up to **10 vehicles** in a single delivery run, even when vehicles originate across multiple auction branches, provided they drop at a single dealership destination.[^bca-transport-logistics]
* **Inspection & Assurance Mechanism (BCA Assured):**
  * Mandatory 40+ point mechanical and electrical condition check on eligible vehicles aged under 8 years and under 120,000 miles, providing pre-sale fault verification.[^bca-buyer-portal]

## Operational Rules & Conditions
* **Account Verification:** Rates and volume status are recalculated on a rolling 12-month purchase volume cycle via the MyBCA dashboard.
* **Ancillary Fees:** Standard V5C processing (£26 incl. VAT) and online bidding platform fees apply across all auction sales.
* **Settlement Timelines:** Post-sale payment required within 2–4 days; collection within 4–5 business days to avoid site storage penalties.
