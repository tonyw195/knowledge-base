# Carwow BuySafe Mechanical Protection, Reserve-Free Bidding & Retail Listings

Carwow operates supplementary buyer risk mitigation under its **BuySafe** scheme alongside fundamental updates to daily auction mechanics and dealer onboarding incentives.[^carwow-buysafe-protection]

## Core Offer & Risk Mitigation (BuySafe Scheme)
* **Incentive Type:** Component Fault Warranty & Self-Collection Risk Mitigation
* **Target Audience:** Independent dealers opting for self-collection or third-party vehicle transport rather than integrated *Carwow Collects* delivery.[^carwow-buysafe-protection]
* **Cost & Tariff:** Flat fee of **£59 + VAT** per vehicle.
* **Coverage Scope:** Grants up to **£7,500 protection per claim** covering approximately 3,000 components (engine, gearbox, electricals, and infotainment) for **5 days** following vehicle delivery or collection.[^carwow-buysafe-protection]
* **Strategic Complement:** Provides a lower-cost protection alternative for buyers who do not need full *Carwow Collects* transport (£25,000 Assured Scheme).

## Auction Mechanics & B2B Commercial Incentives
* **Reserve-Free Bidding Evolution:** Daily auctions transitioned away from traditional displayed seller reserve prices, embedding dynamic retail benchmark valuations (Auto Trader and Brego) directly on auction listings to accelerate bid volumes.[^cardealer-carwow-reserve-removal]
* **Free Used Retail Listings:** Enrolled dealers earn complimentary used-car classified advertising listings on Carwow’s consumer retail marketplace for every vehicle successfully acquired through daily auctions.[^cardealer-carwow-reserve-removal]
* **IMDA Member Onboarding Advantage:** Members of the Independent Motor Dealers Association (IMDA) receive an exclusive **75% discount on their first auction purchase fee** upon joining the platform.[^carwow-imda-partnership]
