# BCA New Trader First-Purchase Buyer Fee Waiver

BCA offers a standard buyer fee waiver program targeting onboarding trade buyers and reactivated accounts [^bca-buyer-fee-promo].

## Offer Details
- **Waiver Scope**: 100% waiver of standard buyer's fee on the first vehicle purchase.
- **Eligibility**:
  - **New Customers**: Where a contract for sale is formed between a Qualifying Buyer and a Seller within thirty (30) calendar days of BCA confirming account registration.
  - **Reactivated Customers**: Qualifying accounts reactivated within designated campaign periods.
- **Application Channel**: The qualifying buyer notifies `Franchisedealer@bca.com` with invoice number, registration, sale name, lot number, and registered business name.

## Exclusions & Constraints
- Valid only on vehicles purchased through BCA Auctions (excludes affiliate platforms within Constellation Automotive Group).
- Ancillary charges remain payable: entry fees, storage/parking fees, V5 handling fees, and BCA Assured inspection report fees.
