# MyBCA Tiered Loyalty Benefits and Inspection Report Subsidies

BCA incentivizes continuous purchasing volume via rolling 12-month tier evaluations that unlock progressive buyer fee discounts, free storage extensions, and subsidized vehicle condition reports [^bca-paying-fees] [^bca-mybca-account].

## Tier Progression Structure
Tiers: **Blue**, **Silver**, **Gold**, **Platinum**, and **Black**.

### Subsidized BCA Assured Inspection Pricing (Excl. VAT)
- **Standard ICE Assured Report**:
  - Non-Account / Blue: £64.50
  - Silver: £58.50
  - Gold: £52.00
  - Platinum: £46.50
  - Black: £36.00 (up to 44% discount)
- **EV/Hybrid Assured Report**:
  - Non-Account / Blue: £70.00
  - Silver: £64.50
  - Gold: £58.00
  - Platinum: £52.00
  - Black: £41.50

### Working Capital & Storage Incentives
- Storage grace periods scale from 2 days (Standard/Blue) up to 5 free storage days before penalty fees apply for Black cardholders.
