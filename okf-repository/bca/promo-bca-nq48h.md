# BCA UKCGR No Quibble Sales 48-Hour Return Scheme

A targeted buyer risk-mitigation incentive for online purchases within UK Car Group (UKCGR) sales [^bca-no-quibble-tc].

## Offer Mechanics
- **Core Benefit**: Full refund of the vehicle hammer price plus the BCA buyer fee upon return.
- **Claim Window**: Notice must be submitted to `customerclaims@bca.com` within 48 hours of vehicle receipt.
- **Usage Restrictions**: Vehicle must be returned to the nearest BCA remarketing centre with less than 350 additional miles on the odometer and in identical condition.

## Eligibility & Tier Restrictions
- Restricted to active mainland UK trade buyers holding **MyBCA Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Black** loyalty cards.
- Non-refundable costs: Outbound/inbound transport logistics, BCA Assured report fees, and parking/storage charges.
