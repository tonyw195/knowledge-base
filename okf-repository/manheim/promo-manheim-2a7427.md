# Manheim Advantage Club Tiers, SureCheck 7-Day Guarantee & Fast Track Logistics

Manheim (the core remarketing division of Cox Automotive UK) operates a structured buyer incentive program based on purchase-volume account tiers, free pre-auction condition guarantees, and nationwide transport logistics.[^manheim-portal]

## Core Buyer Incentives & Volume Tier Structure
* **Incentive Type:** Tiered Volume Buyer Fee Discounts, Inspection Guarantee & Logistics Storage
* **Tiered Account Loyalty Structure:**
  * *Advantage Tier (1–3 vehicles/year):* Baseline registration tier for occasional traders.[^manheim-portal]
  * *Club Tier (4–14 vehicles/year):* Unlocks reduced buyer premium tariffs across physical in-lane and digital sales.[^manheim-portal]
  * *Club+ Tier (15+ vehicles/year):* Maximum reduction in per-vehicle buyer fees, dedicated Buyer Services account management, and discounted SureCheck rates.[^manheim-portal]
* **Account Fee Waivers:** £0 joining fee for new trade accounts and £0 annual account maintenance fees.

## Risk Mitigation & Condition Guarantee (SureCheck Scheme)
* **SureCheck Inspection Scheme:** Pre-sale mechanical condition verification across multiple cover tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, LCV, and dedicated EV (covering high-voltage systems and drivetrains).[^cox-automotive-uk]
* **Fault Rectification Promise:** If a vehicle is found to exhibit a mechanical or electrical defect contrary to the inspection report, Manheim covers the repair cost free of charge.[^manheim-portal]
* **Industry-Leading SLA:** Offers an extended **7-day or 250-mile claims window** following purchase to report condition discrepancies.[^cox-automotive-uk]

## Logistics, Delivery & Storage Concessions
* **Fast Track Delivery:** Nationwide vehicle transport operated by Manheim Vehicle Services, moving single vehicles or full loads directly from any auction centre to mainland UK destinations.[^cox-automotive-uk]
* **Complimentary Site Storage:** Trade buyers receive **3 working days of free vehicle storage** following sale date once payment has cleared before daily storage charges commence.[^manheim-portal]

## Strategic Synergy Note
Manheim's SureCheck system also powers the condition guarantee engine for **Dealer Auction** (their joint venture with Auto Trader), creating a unified remarketing standard across both platforms.
