# Carwow Remarketing Auctions: Zero Subscription & Embedded Settlement Model

## Summary
Carwow operates a direct-to-dealer remarketing auction model designed to compete with traditional physical and digital remarketers by eliminating subscription overheads and integrating real-time stocking finance[^src-carwow-auctions][^src-nextgear-carwow].

## Commercial & Fee Terms
- **Subscription Fee**: £0/month. No recurring dealer subscription or platform access fees[^src-carwow-auctions].
- **Buyer Fee Structure**: Starting from £199 (pegged to be on average 5% below leading competitor benchmarks)[^src-carwow-auctions].
- **Payment Window**: Buyer fees invoiced upon successful vehicle purchase, payable within 7 days[^src-carwow-buyers-tc].

## Auction Dynamics & FinTech Mechanics
- **Extended Bidding Window**: Daily catalog releases open for bidding at 5:00 PM the evening prior, closing at 3:30 PM the following afternoon[^src-carwow-auctions].
- **Bidding Engine**: Autobid increments set at £1 above the next highest bid[^src-carwow-auctions].
- **Carwow Wallet**: Airwallex-powered embedded payment gateway providing single-click split settlement to private sellers, finance houses, and Carwow fees[^src-nextgear-carwow].
- **Integrated Floorplan Financing**: Native API integration with NextGear Capital and LE Capital stocking plans directly inside the platform checkout[^src-nextgear-carwow].
