# Carwow BuySafe Post-Sale Mechanical & Electrical Fault Guarantee

## Summary
Carwow partners with Warranty Solutions Group (WSG) to offer **Carwow BuySafe**, an optional risk mitigation warranty protecting trade buyers who manage their own collection logistics against undetected major mechanical and electrical defects[^src-carwow-buysafe].

## Core Terms & Protection Matrix
- **Cost**: £59 + VAT per vehicle (introductory tiers from £29.99 + VAT)[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- **Maximum Payout**: Up to £7,500 per claim inc. VAT, parts, and labour[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- **Coverage**: Over 3,000 components, including internal engine assemblies, gearbox internals, differentials, superchargers, infotainment, and alternators[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- **Diagnosis Support**: Includes 1 hour of diagnostic labour reimbursement plus up to £500 inc. VAT towards consequential component damage[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- **Payout SLA**: Direct reimbursement within 48 hours of invoice submission to WSG[^src-carwow-buysafe].

## Eligibility Rules
- Vehicles purchased between £5,000 and £80,000[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- Vehicle age and mileage must not exceed 8 years or 80,000 miles[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- Must be opted-in via the Won folder within 24 hours of winning the auction[^src-carwow-buysafe].
- Fault claims must be reported within 5 days post-collection (14-day total policy active window)[^src-carwow-buysafe].
