# Carwow Collects & Assured Transport Protection Scheme

Carwow provides an integrated vehicle logistics, physical appraisal, and buyer protection programme branded as **Carwow Collects** for its dealer auction partners.[^carwow-auctions-portal]

## Core Offer & Incentive Details
* **Incentive Type:** Transport Logistics & Risk Mitigation (Assured Scheme)
* **Buyer Fee Subsidy:** Reduced transportation tariffs for mainland UK vehicle movements managed directly through the Carwow logistics portal.[^carwow-b2b-updates]
* **Risk Guarantee / Protection Value:** Includes the **Assured Scheme**, granting trade buyers up to **£25,000 indemnity protection** per vehicle against undisclosed mechanical or electrical defects, mileage discrepancies (clocking), cloning, and insurance write-off status.[^carwow-b2b-updates]
* **Platform Access Fee:** £0 subscription fee. Dealers pay only standard tiered buyer fees upon winning an auction bid.

## Operational & Eligibility Requirements
* **Eligibility:** Available to all accredited UK dealer partners participating in daily auctions.
* **Appraisal Process:** Transport agents conduct standardized multi-point visual and mechanical condition reports at seller collection point prior to vehicle handover.[^carwow-auctions-portal]
* **Validity:** Ongoing active logistics programme throughout 2026.
