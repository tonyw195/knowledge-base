# Carwow Collects Transport Rate Reduction and Assured Scheme Protection

## Summary
Carwow has upgraded its transport logistics proposition, **Carwow Collects**, pairing discounted nationwide vehicle movements with built-in financial and mechanical loss indemnities under the **Assured Scheme**[^src-carwow-collects].

## Logistics & Pricing Details
- **Base Fee**: £115 for the first 50 miles[^src-carwow-collects].
- **Incremental Rate**: £1.25 per mile for every mile exceeding 50 miles[^src-carwow-collects].
- **Cost Reduction**: Slashes transport fees by an average of £20 for standard 140-mile transits[^src-motortradenews-collects].
- **Included Logistics Overhead**: Absorbs seller-led cancellation charges, fuel/tolls, up to 1 hour driver wait time, and breakdown recovery[^src-carwow-collects].

## Risk Mitigation & Assured Scheme Indemnity
- **Mechanical & Electrical Cover**: Up to £25,000 inc. VAT for significant undisclosed faults across engine, drivetrain, gearbox, cooling, and electrical components. Reporting window: within 13 days of collection[^src-carwow-collects].
- **Financial Loss Indemnity**: Up to £25,000 inc. VAT reimbursement if a vehicle is proven stolen, cloned, clocked, chipped, or an undocumented import. Reporting window: within 90 days of collection[^src-carwow-collects].
- **Eligibility**: Vehicles under 10 years old, under 100,000 miles, and purchased for under £100,000 (standard non-prestige UK models)[^src-carwow-collects].
